# 📊 HR Analytics Dashboard (Power BI Portfolio Project)

## 📌 Project Summary

This project is an end-to-end **HR Analytics Dashboard built using Power BI** designed to analyze employee attrition and uncover key workforce patterns across departments, job roles, salary levels, and employee demographics.

The goal is to help HR teams understand **why employees leave and what factors influence retention** using data-driven insights.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Employee attrition is a major challenge for organizations.  
This project focuses on answering:

- Why are employees leaving the company?
- Which departments have the highest attrition?
- How do salary, job role, and experience affect retention?
- What patterns exist in employee satisfaction and turnover?

---

## 📁 Dataset Overview

The dataset contains employee-level HR information including:

- Employee demographics (Age, Gender, Age Group)  
- Department (HR, R&D, Sales)  
- Job Role and Job Level  
- Salary details (Monthly Income, Salary Slab)  
- Attrition status and Attrition Count  
- Work experience (Years at Company, Total Working Years)  
- Job Satisfaction, Environment Satisfaction, Work-Life Balance  
- Performance Rating, Overtime, Business Travel  

---

## 🧹 Data Preparation

All data transformations were performed using **Power Query Editor**:

- Removed duplicate records  
- Handled missing and null values  
- Cleaned inconsistent and blank entries  
- Removed irrelevant columns  
- Standardized data types  
- Created derived fields like Age Group and Salary Slab  
- Structured data for analysis-ready model  

---

## 🧠 Data Modeling & Calculations

A structured data model was created by connecting employee attributes with HR metrics.

Key calculations include:

- Attrition Count derived from employee attrition status  
- Attrition Rate calculated as ratio of attrition to total employees  
- Aggregations for Age, Salary, and Years at Company  
- Department-level filtering using slicers  

---

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard provides high-level HR insights:

- 👥 Total Employees  
- ❌ Total Attrition  
- 📉 Attrition Rate  
- 🎂 Average Age of Employees  
- 💰 Average Salary  
- 📅 Average Years at Company  

---

## 📈 Dashboard Highlights

The dashboard is fully interactive with slicer-based filtering and includes:

- Department-wise analysis (HR, R&D, Sales)  
- Attrition breakdown by Education Field  
- Attrition distribution by Age Group  
- Attrition trends by Years at Company  
- Job Role vs Attrition analysis  
- Salary slab impact on attrition  
- Job satisfaction matrix across roles  

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

- Sales and R&D departments show higher attrition compared to HR  
- Employees with lower job satisfaction have higher attrition rates  
- Early tenure employees are more likely to leave the organization  
- Salary slab significantly influences employee retention  
- Certain job roles consistently show higher attrition patterns
